Support

Need a hand, or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. A real person reads every one, and we aim to reply within two working days. You'll need a free GitHub account. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details. You can also browse existing issues to see if someone has asked already.

Flying

How do I fly on iPhone Duo?

Turn the Duo sideways and hold it open like a book, with the fold running top to bottom and one hand on each half. Hold it in a relaxed, slightly folded pose, not fully flat. Fold the halves in a little on the way down a wave and the bird folds its wings and dives; open them back to where you started on the way up and it flies off the top. One fold per wave: you never flap. Swoop learns your natural resting angle by itself, and your first flight starts with Flight School, where a guide gull shows each move first.

How do I fly on other iPhones?

Press and hold anywhere on the way down a wave, and let go on the way up. Rather tap? Settings > Wings by > Tapping: one tap folds the wings, the next opens them. Everything in the game, including every goal, bird and achievement, works this way. On a Duo, you can switch to this too: Settings > Play with touch.

What's the trick?

Fold (or hold) on the way down a wave (heavier is faster), open (or let go) on the way up to the top (lighter flies further), and land on the way down of the next wave. Land at a shallow angle there for a Perfect; three in a row is a Tailwind. Folding in the air makes you drop sooner, which is how you pick your landing spot. Nose-diving into a rising wave makes a big splash and costs speed.

The storm keeps catching me

The storm gets faster the longer you fly, so every flight ends eventually. To stay ahead: land Perfects in a row (each one pushes the storm back, and a Tailwind pushes it back a lot), and reach the next lighthouse, which gives you a big lead. The next one is always shown at the top of the screen, and the Route page shows the ones you've passed. When you hear thunder, the storm is close.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Swoop uses small, slow folds of about 30 degrees, roughly one per wave, from a relaxed book pose, well inside the range the Duo's hinge is made for. It never asks you to close the phone, lay it flat or fold fast. Never put anything real on the screen.

Half the screen went blurry for a moment

That's an iOS effect: when you start folding a Duo from fully flat, iOS briefly blurs one half of the screen for about two seconds. It isn't Swoop and no app can switch it off. So Swoop flies your bird on the half that stays sharp (the right half, unless you choose otherwise), and keeps it on that physical half if you turn the phone round. Flight School asks once which half stayed clear on your Duo; to change it later, use Settings > Bird side (Sharp side, Left or Right). Holding the Duo slightly folded rather than fully flat avoids the blur altogether: a slight fold already counts as wings fully open.

The game paused and says "Open your wings to keep flying"

You folded the Duo much further than play needs (below about 55 degrees, well past an L shape). Open it back to your normal book pose and the flight carries on. If you close it fully, the outer screen shows "Open your wings" until you open it again.

It asks me to turn my Duo sideways

Swoop is played with the Duo held like a book, in landscape. In portrait or with the phone standing on a table, it shows how to hold it, and offers Play with touch if you'd rather not fold.

How does the Daily Flight work?

Same waves for everyone that day. Your first try of the day is the one that counts for your result, your streak and the top scores; you can practise as much as you like afterwards. Tap Share to send your result.

How do I unlock birds and skies?

Open Goals: you have three at a time. Complete all three to go up a level (there are 15). Eight birds, five skies and coloured trails unlock as you level up (the Gull and the Golden Hour sky are yours from the start). Birds only change the look; every bird flies exactly the same. There are no purchases, ever.

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Swoop plays sound even with the silent switch on, and mixes with your music. To make it follow the silent switch, turn on Respect silent switch in Settings. Sound has its own switch, and Haptics can be Off, Normal or Strong.

Reduce Motion

Swoop follows Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion: camera shake and screen bloom are turned off. Swoop's Settings shows whether it's on.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your longest flight and your Daily Flight go to the top scores (Game Center) and achievements are reported as you earn them. Swoop works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Will I have to pay again later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. Updates are free.

Family Sharing?

Yes, Swoop is available through Family Sharing.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it. Settings > Reset progress starts you over on purpose.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • whether you were flying with the fold or with touch;
  • the mode you were playing (Flight or Daily Flight);
  • what happened, and what you expected;
  • a screen recording, if you can. It helps a lot.
