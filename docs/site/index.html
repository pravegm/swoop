How it plays

On iPhone Duo

Hold the Duo open like a book, one hand on each half. Fold it in a little and the bird tucks its wings and dives down the face of a swell, picking up speed. Open it at the bottom and the bird soars off the next crest. Small, slow folds, one wave at a time.

On every other iPhone

Press and hold anywhere to tuck, and let go to soar. Same sea, same leaderboards. The fold is the best way to fly, but nobody is left out.

Outfly the storm

A storm front follows you from behind, slate cloud and slanting rain, and it gets faster the longer you fly. Ahead, across the fold, the sky stays clear and golden. Every Perfect skim pushes the storm back, and every lighthouse you pass buys you a lead.

One move, a lot of skill

  • Tuck on the way down: heavier is faster. Open on the way up: lighter flies further.
  • Land on the next downslope for a Perfect: a white ring, a chime that climbs with your chain, a little extra speed.
  • Three Perfects in a row catch a Tailwind: a golden trail and a burst of speed.
  • Nose-dive into a rising wave and you'll know about it: a big, heavy splash.

Ways to play

  • Flight: endless, a new sea every time.
  • Daily Flight: the same sea for everyone today, one scored try. Keep your streak and share your result.
  • Flocks: goals three at a time. Rank up from Fledgling to Storm Rider and unlock eight birds and five skies. Every bird flies the same; only the look changes.

Kind to your hinge

Swoop plays from a relaxed book pose with short, slow folds, about one wave every two or three seconds. It never asks you to close your phone, lay it flat or fold fast. Nothing real ever goes on the screen.

Pay once. That's it.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking and no data collected. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Every sound is made live in the app as you fly. Game Center is optional.
