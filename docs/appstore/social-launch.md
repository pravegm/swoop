## 3. Video storyboards

### 3A. Social teaser: "These are your wings" (15 s, vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, sound on)

**Shot:** the founder's real iPhone Duo held open like a book in two hands, filmed **from just behind and above the right shoulder**, so the hands, the fold and both screens are all in frame. Soft daylight, plain background. Phone sound **on** (clip mic near the speaker). Real hands are fine here (not an App Store asset).

| Time | Picture | On-screen text (big, top third, clear of the phone) | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 | Cold open: the title screen, the big bird's wings spanning both halves. The hands fold the phone in a little and the bird's wings fold **with them**, then open | **These are your wings.** | wind, soft |
| 1.5–4.0 | 3-2-1, the drop. A gentle tuck down the first swell, the wind rising | **Fold to dive.** | wind rising in pitch |

Rules: no price in the video (it goes in the post text), no fast or repeated folding, never closed or slammed, the strokes small and at the pace of the waves. Export H.264, ≤ 60 MB (fits X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, TikTok).

### 3B. App Preview: "Fold to dive" (optional for 1.0; 25 s, landscape 1920 × 886, 30 fps)

Apple's rules: 15–30 s; captures of the app only (**no hands, no people, no device frame**); no prices; autoplays muted, so every beat has a caption on screen ≥ 1.5 s; poster frame chosen by us. The Duo's book canvas (aspect 1.42) doesn't fill 1920 × 886 (2.17), so scale the inner screen to 886 px tall (about 1,260 px wide), centre it, and put captions in the dusk bands left and right. Never crop into the game.

**Ships in 1.0 only if** it's recorded and checked by code freeze (Mon 16 Nov). Previews lock with the version, so a late one waits for 1.0.1. It needs a screen-recording step in CI (Squish has `preview.yml`; Swoop doesn't yet: `DECISIONS.md` §2).

## 5. Don'ts

- No "break your hinge", "fold as fast as you can" or wear jokes. No fast or repeated folding on camera, and never closing the phone shut as part of play.
- No competitor names (Tiny Wings, Alto, Valley Run, Flappy Bird), not even as "like X but".
- No "share to unlock", follow-for-codes giveaways or bought engagement.
- "iPhone Duo" only in referential use ("for iPhone Duo"), never as part of our product's name; no Apple logos.
- No price inside videos (keep it in the post text).
