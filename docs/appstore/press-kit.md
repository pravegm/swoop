## The pitch (one paragraph)

**Swoop turns the iPhone Duo into a pair of wings.** Hold the Duo open like a book, one hand on each half. Fold it in a little and the bird tucks and dives down the face of an ocean swell, picking up speed; open it at the bottom and it soars off the next crest. Land on the next downslope for a Perfect skim, chain three for a golden Tailwind, and keep ahead of the storm front that chases you across the sea, faster the longer you fly. Named lighthouses mark the way. There's an endless Flight mode, a seeded Daily Flight that's the same for everyone, and goals that rank you up from Fledgling to Storm Rider and unlock eight birds and five skies. It also plays on every other iPhone: press and hold to tuck. £1.99 / $1.99, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected, rated 4+.

## Key features (for writers who want bullets)

- **The phone's halves are the wings.** Fold the Duo a little and the bird tucks; open it and the bird spreads its wings. The title bird's wings span both screens and fold *with you*.
- **Analogue, not a switch.** How far you tuck sets how heavy the bird is. The skill is rhythm: tuck on the way down, open on the way up.
- **Perfects and Tailwinds.** Land on a downslope at a shallow angle for a Perfect skim: a white ring, a chime that climbs a scale, a little extra speed. Three in a row catch a golden Tailwind.
- **A storm with a face.** Slate cloud and slanting rain behind you, clear gold sky ahead across the fold. Perfects push it back; lighthouses buy you a lead. When it catches you, the bird just tucks its head and rides the swell.
- **One more go.** Runs last one to four minutes. The Daily Flight is the same sea for everyone, with one scored try and a streak.
- **Kind to the hinge.** Short, unhurried strokes of 30–36° from a relaxed book pose. It never asks you to close the phone, lay it flat or fold fast.
