### Description
```
Swoop turns iPhone Duo into a pair of wings. Hold the Duo open like a book, one hand on each half: fold it in a little and the bird tucks and dives down the face of an ocean swell; open it at the bottom and the bird soars off the next crest. The two halves of the phone fold with the bird.

The hinge is an analogue control, not a switch. How far you tuck sets how heavy the bird is, so the skill is rhythm: tuck on the way down, open on the way up, and land on the next downslope for a Perfect skim. Three in a row catch a golden Tailwind. Behind you, a storm front gets faster the longer you fly; ahead, across the fold, the sky stays gold. Named lighthouses mark the way.

Endless flights of one to four minutes, a seeded Daily Flight that is the same for everyone, goals that unlock eight birds and five skies, and Game Center leaderboards. Any iPhone can play by pressing and holding. One price, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected.
```

### Helpful Details
```
Optimized for iPhone Duo: built for book pose on the inner display, with a pose card in portrait or tabletop and a pause card on the outer display when closed. Uses the public iOS 27.1 hinge API with short, slow strokes of about 30 degrees from a relaxed, self-calibrating book pose; it never asks players to close the phone, lay it flat or fold fast. A small studio: every sound is synthesized in the app. Also from us: Squish, and Brim (launching 17 Nov).
```

**Why this copy:**
- **New hardware first:** the opening line, and "Optimized for iPhone Duo" in Helpful Details (Apple's Duo guidance asks for exactly that phrase).
- **The fantasy in one image:** the phone's halves are the wings and fold with the bird. No other phone can do it.
- **Craft:** an analogue tuck, a physics skill (rhythm on the swells), synthesized sound.
- **Replayability:** endless runs, the Daily Flight, goals and unlocks, leaderboards.
- **Accessibility and reach:** press-and-hold on every iPhone. No VoiceOver claim, because that label isn't earned (`accessibility.md`).
- **Kind to the hardware:** answers the wear question before an editor asks it.
- **No ads:** the last line.
