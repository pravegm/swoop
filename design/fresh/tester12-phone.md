# Tester 12 — iPhone 16 Pro, first session of Swoop: Tuck & Soar (£1.99)

26, marketing, I play on my commute (Royal Match, Alto's Odyssey, Crossy Road, Wordle). No foldable.

## Screen by screen
1. **Home.** Lovely. Sunset, a big gull seen from above, a storm cloud on the left, a little gull skimming the sea. "Hold anywhere. The wings fold." One big yellow Fly button that says "Next: Gull Point · 4 km". I know where to tap. Looks like the screenshots, good start.
2. **Tutorial opens.** Same sky, big gull up top, a wavy line over the water. A little dark rectangle with a circle in it floats in the middle with no words. A light switch? A phone? I waited.
3. **"Hold anywhere"** appears under it, plus a Skip button. Right, so that box is a phone and the circle is my thumb. Fine.
4. **I hold.** The big gull up top folds its wings into a dart shape and the box goes yellow. That's satisfying, the top bird copies what my finger does. I get "tuck".
5. **New scene, bright daytime.** Gull at the top of a long glowing slope, yellow arrows pointing downhill, "Hold". There's a little yellow icon above the gull's back, like an open book. No idea what that is.
6. **"Keep holding."** I think I let go too early. Nothing bad happened, it just told me again. Gentle, I liked that.
7. **"Let go."** Racing up the wave with spray and a yellow trail, and a gold ghost bird outline ahead on the crest. Following the ghost makes sense.
8. **"Land here."** Flying through the air. The words float on the slope, but there's no marker on the water so I'm only guessing where "here" is.
9. **Landed.** A white ring splashes round the gull. The filename says this was a "perfect", but nothing on screen said so. No word, no sound I'd notice on the Tube with sound off. I didn't know I'd done well.
10. **"One per wave."** One what? One hold? One perfect? The glowing slope is there again, plus a blue version of the book icon with up arrows at the bottom of the dip. I guess it means let go there, but I'm not sure.
11. **"Stay ahead."** Calm sea, no storm in sight. Ahead of what?
12. **A bar appears at the top**: storm icon on the left, my bird icon in the middle, and a lighthouse on the right labelled Gull Point. OK, it's a race: storm behind me, lighthouse in front.
13. **Same as 12.** Nothing moved. It felt like the tutorial had stalled.
14. **Countdown "2"** with "Hold going down · let go going up." That's the clearest line in the whole game, and it only shows up after the tutorial. If I'd seen it on screen 3 I'd have understood straight away.
15. **The real run.** Diving down a wave at sunset, "61 m" top right, "NEXT Gull Point · 4.0 km", and the storm bar now has a "Storm" label. Gorgeous, very Alto's.
16. **"Flight over"** at 12.8 km. The storm cloud is right on my tail and the rain is over me. It's clear the storm caught me, but it felt sudden. The bar wasn't on screen at that moment, so I couldn't see it closing in.
17. **Results.** "YOUR FIRST FLIGHT. You made it past Saltlight!" plus a "New lighthouse" badge, 12.8 km, "Kestrel Head is 2.2 km further." and "Perfect landings push the storm back." That last line is where I finally learned what perfects are for. Also "New bird: Tern, Level 2" with Fly as Tern. A nice reward hit.
18. **Home again.** The tagline now reads "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses." Why wasn't that on the first home screen? There's also a Daily Flight (27 Oct, "Same waves for everyone · 1 try"), which is very Wordle and I love it. Plus Goals, Route and Birds (New).
19. **Route.** A lovely map: a pier, then lighthouses at 4, 9, 15, 22, 30 and 38 km, with the storm at the start. Gull Point and Saltlight are ticked and Kestrel Head is next. Now I have a reason to go again.
20. **Goals.** Level 2 of 15, "Puddle Hopper", 2 of 3 done. Unlocks are birds, skies and trails. The third goal is cut off at the bottom and I'd have to scroll.

## The four questions
1. **Finger:** press and hold anywhere while the gull is heading downhill, which tucks the wings so it dives fast. Let go as it starts climbing the next wave so it launches. Then land smoothly on a downslope. It's one hold per wave, I think.
2. **Storm:** a dark rain cloud chasing me from behind. The bar at the top shows how close it is. If it catches me, the flight's over. Perfect landings push it back.
3. **Where:** east along a coast of lighthouses (Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9, Kestrel Head 15, then on to 38+). Each lighthouse is a checkpoint and a bragging marker.
4. **Success / failure:** success is distance, passing lighthouses, perfect landings, and ticking goals to unlock birds. Failure is the storm catching you. I never saw any other way to die, so a bad landing just costs speed.

## Verdicts
- **Finished and premium?** Mostly yes. The art, the soft colours and the results card all look like a proper Apple-featured indie, and there are no ads or popups. It loses a bit for the tutorial: wordless first screen, mystery book icon, "One per wave", and a stalled storm step.
- **Own thing or Tiny Wings knock-off?** Honestly, the core loop is Tiny Wings: hold on downhills, release on uphills, something chasing you. The seaside, the lighthouses, the daily seed and the folding-wings bird at the top give it its own personality. I'd describe it to friends as "Tiny Wings at the seaside, but prettier". That's not an insult, but it's not new.
- **Short-changed without a foldable?** No. Nothing mentioned foldables. "Tuck & Soar" works fine with a thumb, and the big top bird folding its wings is a nice touch on a normal phone.
- **Confused me:** 2 (wordless box icon), 5/10 (the yellow and blue "book" icon), 8 (where is "here"?), 9 (no "Perfect!" feedback), 10 ("One per wave" of what?), 11–13 ("Stay ahead" of nothing, then a static step), 16 (death felt sudden, the bar wasn't visible), 17 vs 20 (did I unlock Tern or not? Goals still says 2 of 3). Numbering follows my list above, where screen 16 is the s1-15b file.
- **Tell friends?** I'd tell the ones who liked Tiny Wings or Alto's, mainly for the Daily Flight ("I got 12.8 km, beat me"). I wouldn't rave about it as something new.

## Scores
- Understood: 7/10
- Feels finished: 8/10
- Keep playing: 7/10
- Recommend: 6/10
