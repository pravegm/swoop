## My answers
1. **Hands:** Hold the phone open like a book. Fold it a little (a "tuck") as you go over the top of a wave to dive, keep it folded all the way down, then open it at the bottom to launch into the air. Then try to come down while the next wave is going downhill.
2. **The storm:** A dark rain cloud chasing me from behind (from the right). It's the bar at the top. If it catches me, the flight is over. Perfect landings push it back.
3. **Where:** Leftwards along a coast, from lighthouse to lighthouse (Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9 km, Kestrel Head 15 km...). Each one I reach goes on my Route, and the next flight aims at the next one.
4. **Success / failure:** Success is going far, reaching new lighthouses and getting lots of Perfects. Failure is the storm catching me, and opening too soon in a dive wastes it. There's no real "game over" moment, just "Caught X km short".

## What delighted me
- The big gull on the first screen with its wings across both halves. Folding the phone = folding wings is really charming.
- The diving streak and splash look gorgeous, and so does the sunset with the storm rolling in.
- Being told "You opened too soon" made failing feel friendly, not punishing.
- 12.83 km and two lighthouses on my first go felt good. The rank-up and new bird were a nice surprise.

## Play again tomorrow?
Probably yes, for the Daily Flight and to reach Kestrel Head. It's quick and the fold is a genuinely new feeling. My worry is whether the fold-open rhythm gets tiring, and I didn't fully know what "Perfect" timing feels like.
