## The four questions
1. **Finger**: Hold while the bird is heading downhill on a wave (wings tuck, it speeds up). Let go at the bottom so it launches up and out. One hold per wave.
2. **Storm**: A rain cloud chasing from behind (left). The top bar shows how close it is. Perfect landings push it back. If it catches you, the run ends.
3. **Where**: Along a coast of lighthouses: Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9, Kestrel Head 15, Old Harry 22, Wren Ness 30, then endless.
4. **Success/failure**: Success is distance, reaching lighthouses (they get ticked on the Route), and Perfects (landing on a downslope). Failure is the storm catching you. Every run ends that way, so "success" means getting further than last time.

## Fun and addictive, or a pretty Tiny Wings knock-off?
Honestly the core *is* Tiny Wings: hold downhill, release uphill, land on the slope for a Perfect, outrun a timer (night there, storm here). Anyone who's played Tiny Wings will spot it in 10 seconds, and TikTok comments will say so.
What makes it its own thing:
- Ocean waves instead of hills, with spray and a wake. It looks like Alto's Odyssey on water, and it's gorgeous.
- The wing-fold, which you see from above. "Tuck & Soar" is a nice identity.
- Lighthouses as named checkpoints with a Route map. That gives me a destination, which Tiny Wings' islands never quite did for me.
- Daily Flight (same sea for everyone, one try) is Wordle-ish and shareable. A unlockable bird collection (Tern) is a Crossy Road-style hook.
It does look addictive: "2.17 km before Kestrel Head" is the exact itch that makes me press Fly again. But mechanically it's not new. The storm needs to feel scarier, and the birds need to play differently, or it stays "pretty Tiny Wings".

## Does it feel finished?
Mostly. The art, the HUD and the Route screen feel premium. But:
- Text truncated with "..." on the results card (16), three times.
- The tutorial card hides the bird (11-13).
- "Land in the ring ahead" with no ring visible (08).
- Mystery meters (05, 10).
- Getting caught doesn't look like getting caught (15b).
It feels like it's about 90% done.

## Short-changed without a foldable?
No. Nothing told me I was missing anything; it plays fine one-handed on a normal phone. The wing "fold" is just the hold. If there's a foldable mode, I never knew, so I don't feel cheated.
