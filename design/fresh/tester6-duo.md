# Swoop: Tuck & Soar, first session (tester 6, iPhone Duo)

I'm a 36-year-old PM who plays Things, Monument Valley and Alto's Odyssey. I paid £1.99 and played one session (s1-01 to s1-18; s1-12 is missing).

## The basics, in my own words
- **Hands:** I hold the Duo open like a book, and the two halves are the gull's wings. Folding it partway is a "tuck", which makes me dive down a wave. Opening it at the bottom of the wave flings me up into the air (s1-02 to s1-07).
- **The storm:** a rain cloud chasing me from the right. The bar at the top shows how far behind me it is (s1-08, s1-15).
- **Where I'm going:** left, along a coast of lighthouses: Gull Point at 4 km, Saltlight at 9 km, Kestrel Head at 15 km, and on from there (s1-18).
- **Success:** a "Perfect" landing on a downslope pushes the storm back, and each lighthouse I reach goes on my Route. Distance is the score.
- **Failure:** the storm catches me and the run ends. My first run: 12.83 km, 11 Perfects, caught 2.17 km short of Kestrel Head (s1-16).

## First impression
This is close to premium, but it isn't finished. The art is lovely. The sunset sky, the soft sun, the big top-down albatross on the home screen and the spray off the wave (s1-01, s1-07, s1-11) are Alto-grade mood. The core idea, fold to dive and open to soar, is clever. It's the first Duo game I've seen where the hinge feels like the reason the game exists. The countdown cue "Wings open, hold steady" (s1-14) is a nice touch.

Several things break the spell, though.

**Unfinished or inconsistent:**
- **s1-18 Route:** two lighthouses are literally named "Lighthouse 5" and "Lighthouse 6". That's placeholder text in a paid app. Gull Point, which I reached, doesn't appear on the map, even though the card says "2 lighthouses reached". The bottom half of the screen is empty dark blue with a visible seam (around y=950).
- **s1-01, s1-17:** there's a tiny yellow sliver before "Outfly the storm…" and "Next: Kestrel Head". It looks like a broken or clipped icon.
- **s1-04:** the screen says "That's a tuck" with a tick, but the fold gauge needle and the book diagram haven't moved from s1-02/03. The feedback doesn't match the visual.
- **Tutorial pacing:** the step dots jump around. s1-10 ("rhythm") drops the Flight School card entirely and shows "Go" plus the in-game banner. After that, "Step 7 of 7" is used for three different messages (s1-08, s1-11, s1-13). It feels like the tutorial was stitched together.
- **Mixed messages:** "A storm is chasing you out to sea" (s1-11) against "Reach the next lighthouse" (s1-13). Am I heading out to sea or along the coast?
- **The run HUD doesn't add up:** the tutorial distance chip sits at "4.00 km" across s1-08, s1-11 and s1-13, so it doesn't seem to count. In the run it's 60 m on the left and 3.94 km on the right (s1-15). Two numbers and two units is a lot to parse mid-dive.
- **s1-16 Results:** the headline is "Reached Saltlight!", but the next line says "Caught 2.17 km short of Kestrel Head". I had to read it twice to tell if I'd won or lost. The rank-up card then sets "Fly 3 km in one flight" as a new goal right after I flew 12.83 km, which feels like it was written blind to what I'd just done. The mini route strip puts my bird just past the first lighthouse, not past the second.
- **Contrast:** the step 4 card (s1-07) is a muddy grey that fades into the bright sky, and "Open now!" is hard to read at the most important moment. "Faster!" (s1-05) is white on pale peach. "Skip" disappears on the sunset (s1-08, s1-13).

**Cluttered:**
- **s1-17 Home:** four "New" badges, a Daily Flight card, Goals/Route/Birds, Leaderboards and Settings all crowd onto the second visit. The first home screen (s1-01) was calm, and this one shouts. The tagline also changes from white to yellow between the two visits.
- **s1-16:** the results card has eight separate lines of copy before you reach "Fly again". Plus the rank-up modal.

**What I'd change first:**
1. Name every lighthouse and fix the Route screen (show Gull Point, fill or crop the empty half). That's where the "half-baked" feeling comes from.
2. Make the results headline say one thing ("New lighthouse: Saltlight · 12.83 km"), with the near-miss as a quiet second line. Make the next goals sensible.
3. Make the tutorial fold gauge actually react, and keep the step numbering honest.
4. Cut the home screen badges down to one, and fix the broken icon sliver.
5. Fix contrast on the tutorial cards.

## Comparisons
- **Alto's Odyssey:** the art direction and colour are in the same league, and the input is more novel than Alto's single tap. Alto is far quieter, though. There are almost no words on screen, the HUD is minimal and the menus are restrained. Swoop explains too much and labels too much.
- **Monument Valley:** MV teaches through the world itself, with no step counters or text boxes, and every screen feels hand-finished. Swoop's seven-step text tutorial and "Lighthouse 5" placeholder are exactly what MV would never ship. On clarity, MV wins easily. Swoop's goal is clear, but its feedback screens aren't.

## Recommendation
Yes, to a friend with a Duo, with a caveat. It's the best showcase of the fold I've found, it's cheap, and one run hooked me. I'd tell them it looks like a 1.0 that needs one more polish pass, especially the menus. I'd wait to recommend it to anyone without a Duo, or as "premium".

## Scores
- I understood it: **8/10**. The loop is clear by the end of Flight School. The results and route screens muddied it.
- It feels premium/finished: **6/10**. Beautiful in-game, but placeholder names, a broken icon and inconsistent copy are visible.
- I'd keep playing: **7/10**. The tuck-and-soar feel plus the lighthouse goals are a good hook. The daily flight helps.
