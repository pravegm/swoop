# Swoop: Tuck & Soar - first session (tester 13, iPhone Duo)

Persona: 61, retired teacher, plays Wordle and solitaire, not a gamer. Paid £1.99 and held the Duo open like a book.

## Screen by screen

- **s1-01 (home)**: Lovely, calm title screen. The big gull's wings run across both halves, and "Hold it like a book. These are your wings." told me straight away how to hold it. Problems: the pale-blue line "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses." is low contrast over the bright sky and hard to read at arm's length. The two round icons bottom right (a numbered list and a cog) have no labels, so I don't know what the list one is. "Next: Gull Point · 4 km" doesn't mean much yet, since nothing has told me what Gull Point is.
- **s1-02 (school, hold)**: "Hold it open" with a little open-book picture, which is clear. But there are two gulls: a big one seen from above at the top and a small side-on one on the wave. Which one is me? Nobody says. The top gull seems to be a picture of my hands. I only guessed that.
- **s1-03 (fold a little)**: "Fold a little". The book picture looks almost the same as the one in s1-02; only the dashed outline changed from blue to yellow. "A little" is how much? There's no angle shown and no arrow showing which way to move.
- **s1-04 (wings done)**: The big gull's wings tucked into a dart, which is nice and clearly my doing. The book icon went beige and the text vanished. Was that a success? There's no tick, no "Good" and no sound cue on screen. The page dots still show step 1 of 6.
- **s1-05 (dive)**: The big top gull has gone, the scene changes to a sunny wave, and it just says "Fold". The page dots jumped from step 1 to step 3. The "Fold" icon looks the same as the "Fold a little" icon, so is this a bigger fold? A tiny yellow book floats on the gull's back; I assume it's a gauge but it's very small.
- **s1-06 (dive fail)**: "Opened too soon". I was never told when I was supposed to open, so it felt like being marked wrong on a question that hadn't been asked. It doesn't say what opening too soon cost me.
- **s1-07 (soar)**: "Open" over an empty dashed box, with the gull at the bottom of a wave climbing up. From the pictures I worked out: fold at the top, open at the bottom. No screen says that in words. The dashed box floats in the sky, disconnected from anything.
- **s1-08 (land)**: "Land here" floats on its own in the water on the left. The yellow ring it seems to point to is half off the screen at the bottom-left edge, partly behind the Skip button. There's another ring around the gull itself. Two rings, and the label next to neither.
- **s1-09 (perfect)**: "Perfect" in big yellow letters, with a down-arrow and dotted line over the next ring. This is the clearest teaching screen in the whole thing. It should have come before s1-08, not after.
- **s1-10 (rhythm)**: "One fold per wave". That's the most important rule and it gets one small line of text and no picture. I took it to mean don't flap or pump, do one fold per wave.
- **s1-11 (storm 1)**: There's nothing on this screen: no text, no storm, just the gull on a wave. It's step 6 of 6 and looks unfinished, or like the text hasn't loaded.
- **s1-12 (storm 2)**: A bar appears at the top with a gull marker, a yellow-to-red section and a cloud icon, plus the lighthouse "Gull Point" on the left and "Stay ahead". The bar has no lighthouse on it, so I can't tell how far away the goal is. The storm itself isn't on screen, only an icon. "Gull Point" is pale text over a pale sky.
- **s1-13 (storm 3)**: "Land a Perfect". A perfect what? It reads like a word is missing. Otherwise it's the same screen as s1-12, and the bar hasn't moved.
- **s1-13b (storm push)**: The gull is airborne over a big wave and it says "Stay ahead" again. The bar is still identical. What does "push" mean here? Not shown.
- **s1-14 (countdown)**: The countdown "2" with "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses. / Wings open, hold steady". It's readable and it's a good recap. The second line has no full stop, so the two sentences don't match. Now I can actually see the storm cloud on the right. Good.
- **s1-15 (run)**: "64 m" big at top left and "NEXT Gull Point · 4.0 km" under it. Is 64 m how far I've gone or how high I am? It doesn't say. If I've flown 64 m, shouldn't Gull Point say 3.9 km? A pink streak comes out of the storm cloud behind the gull. Is that my trail or lightning? It isn't clear. The bar now has the word "Storm" under the icon, which it should have had in the tutorial.
- **s1-15b (caught)**: Suddenly it says 12.8 km and "Flight over", with the gull lying on the water under the cloud. It doesn't say why, so I had to assume the storm caught me. The HUD bar and the "Next" lighthouse have disappeared. I never saw myself pass a lighthouse; none of the frames show it.
- **s1-15c (flight over)**: The storm swallows the screen. It looks broken in two places. There's a hard vertical edge in the sky near the left side, bright on one side and grey on the other. To the right of the cloud there's a smeared horizontal streak, like stretched pixels. On a paid game that looks like a glitch.
- **s1-16 (results)**: Two cards, one on each half, which works well on the Duo. "You made it past Gull Point and Saltlight!" is clear and pleasing, and so are the route line and the big "12.8 km". But it says "NEW LIGHTHOUSE" (singular) after I passed two. "2.2 km to go" and "Kestrel Head is 2.2 km further." say the same thing twice. There's a big empty gap above "Fly again". The right card says "New bird: Tern · Level 2". I didn't know there were levels or birds, so what did I do to earn it? "Or fold and open to fly again" is a nice touch.
- **s1-17 (home again)**: New buttons have appeared: Goals, Route, Birds, Daily Flight. The "New" badge on Birds squeezes the word down so it's misaligned with Goals and Route. The two unlabeled round icons now sit on top of the wave's spray art and look stuck on. The "hold it like a book" line has gone, which is fine now.
- **s1-18 (route)**: This finally answers "where am I going": a coastline of lighthouses, with ticks on Gull Point 4 km and Saltlight 9 km, Kestrel Head 15 km marked "Next", then "and on...". It's clear and I liked it. But "Kestrel Head / 15 km · Next" sits right on the fold, so the hinge cuts through the words. The solid yellow line stops at Saltlight while my gull sits further on along the dashed line. On this map the storm is on the left and I travel right, but in the game the gull faces left with the storm on the right. That's mirrored, and it took me a moment. The bottom half of the screen is empty.
- **s1-19 (goals)**: "Level 2 of 15 · next: New trail: Coral" is a mouthful, with two colons in one heading. "Puddle Hopper", "Fledgling" and "Spray Chaser": are those ranks or rewards? I'm already Level 2 and 2 of 3 goals are done after one flight, so what got me to Level 2? It isn't explained. The unlocks list is cut off at the bottom ("Level 8 ... Tern Turner" is clipped), and the left half's lower part is empty.

## The four questions

1. **What do my hands do?** Hold the Duo open like a book: those are the wings, and I glide. Fold it partly shut to tuck the wings and dive down a wave, then open it again to soar up the next one. From the pictures it seems to be fold at the top and open at the bottom, but no screen says that in words. I do NOT flap repeatedly: "One fold per wave" (s1-10). How far to fold, I still don't know. The icons for "Fold a little" and "Fold" look the same.
2. **What is the storm?** A dark rain cloud chasing me from behind. The bar at the top seems to show the gap between me and it. If it reaches me the flight ends (s1-15b). The game never actually says "the storm caught you"; I inferred it. During the tutorial it's just an icon, not a visible threat.
3. **Where am I going?** Along a coast from lighthouse to lighthouse: Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9 km, Kestrel Head 15 km, Old Harry 22 km, Wren Ness 30 km, "and on...". I only properly understood this on the Route screen (s1-18), after my first flight, not before it.
4. **What is success and failure?** Success: going further, passing lighthouses, landing "Perfect" in the yellow ring, and finishing goals to unlock birds, skies and trails. Failure: the storm catches you and it's "Flight over". "Opened too soon" is some sort of mistake, but what it costs me isn't shown.

## Verdicts

**Finished and premium?** Mostly. The art, the home screen, the results cards and the Route map feel like a proper paid app, and the results card is genuinely pleasing. The tutorial lets it down. It's a series of one-word labels ("Fold", "Open", "Land here") over icons that look nearly identical. It has one blank step (s1-11), a rule shown as a failure before it's taught (s1-06), and a broken-looking storm transition (s1-15c). That part feels like a draft.

**Would I keep playing?** Probably a few more goes. I want to reach Kestrel Head and it's only 2.2 km away, which is a good hook. I'm not sure I'm doing the fold right, though, and if I keep getting "Opened too soon" without knowing why, I'll go back to Wordle.

**Single most confusing moment:** s1-05 to s1-06. I'm told "Fold", the icon looks the same as "Fold a little", and then I'm told "Opened too soon" when nobody had said when to open. That's the core move of the game and it's taught by telling me off.

**Top 5 things to fix (most important first):**
1. **Teach the rhythm in plain words before marking me wrong (s1-05, s1-06, s1-07, s1-10).** Say something like "Fold at the top of a wave, open at the bottom", then show it, then test it. Make the fold icons clearly different: show how far to fold, with an arrow and a fill or angle gauge, instead of a recoloured dashed outline that looks the same each time (s1-02 vs s1-03 vs s1-05).
2. **Say why the flight ended and fix the storm rendering (s1-15b, s1-15c).** Use "The storm caught you" instead of a bare "Flight over", and keep the storm-gap bar on screen as it closes. Fix the hard vertical edge in the sky and the smeared streak beside the cloud in s1-15c. It looks like a bug.
3. **Fix the empty and half-finished tutorial steps (s1-11, s1-04, s1-13).** s1-11 has no text at all. s1-04 needs a clear "Well done" or tick. "Land a Perfect" needs its missing word. The page dots jump from step 1 to step 3 (s1-04 to s1-05).
4. **Make the landing target readable (s1-08).** Put "Land here" next to the ring it means. Don't let the target ring sit half off-screen behind the Skip button. Either show one ring, or explain which ring is me and which is the target. Show s1-09's arrow version first.
5. **Make the distance and progress info make sense (s1-12, s1-15, s1-18).** Put the lighthouse on the storm bar so I can see the goal as well as the threat. Label "64 m" (distance flown?) and make "Next: 4.0 km" count down. On Route, move "Kestrel Head 15 km · Next" off the fold and run the solid line up to my gull. Also on results and goals (s1-16, s1-19): use "NEW LIGHTHOUSES" when I pass two, explain how I got to Level 2, and stop the unlocks list being clipped.
