# Tester 8 (Duo) - retired, 61, solitaire and word games

## Screen by screen

**01 Home.** Lovely picture, a big gull, a sunset. "Hold your Duo like a book. These are your wings." That's clear and rather charming. Big yellow "Fly" button, so I'd press that. "Outfly the storm" - I'd guess it means fly faster than the storm.

**03 Wings (step 2 of 7).** "These are your wings. Fold a little." The little drawing of the phone and the dial saying fold/open help. I'd close the book a bit. The dots at the top tell me there are 7 steps, which is reassuring.

**04 Wings done.** "Wings folded!" with a tick, and the big gull tucked its wings in. Satisfying. I get it: closing the phone = tucking the wings.

**05 Dive (step 3).** "Stay folded all the way down." Down what? I think down the wave. There's a little bar saying "Faster!" over the bird but I don't know what I'm meant to do with that bar.

**06 Dive fail.** "You opened too soon. Stay folded." Fine, kindly told off. I'd keep it closed longer. But I wasn't sure *how* far down "all the way" is.

**07 Soar (step 4).** "Open at the bottom. Fly!" So: close going down, open at the bottom of the dip. Now it clicks a bit, like a swing.

**08 Land (step 5).** "Land in the ring ahead." There's a faint ring on the water. I'd have missed it if it hadn't said so - it's pale.

**09 Perfect.** Big yellow PERFECT. "Land on a downslope = Perfect." I had to think about "downslope" - the side of the wave going down, I suppose. The "=" sign feels like maths homework.

**10 Rhythm (step 6).** "One fold per wave. Never flap." I like "never flap" - don't keep opening and shutting. There's another bar with a blue blob and three tiny dots under the bird. No idea what those mean.

**11-13 Storm (step 7).** "A storm is chasing you." then "Perfects push the storm back." then "Fly to Gull Point - 4 km." A bar appears at the top with my bird and a cloud at the end. I worked out: cloud on the right, me in the middle, keep away from the red end. Three messages in a row came quickly.

**14 Countdown.** Big "2", "Wings open, hold steady". Clear. Plus a second message at the bottom repeating the goal - fine, but two messages at once.

**15 Flying.** "63 m", "Next lighthouse: Gull Point 3.94 km", storm bar, "Storm behind you. Don't let it catch you." The bird is diving. The message box sits right on top of my bird, which I didn't like - I want to see the bird.

**15b Caught.** "The storm caught you" at 12.83 km. A big grey cloud and rain. Clear enough that it's over.

**16 Results.** Confusing at first: in big yellow it says "Reached Saltlight!" (sounds like I won) but straight underneath "The storm caught you 2.17 km before Kestrel Head" (sounds like I lost). So did I win or lose? I think: I got past two lighthouses then got caught. Then a second box: "You ranked up! New bird: Tern. Rank 2 - Puddle Hopper" and a list of goals that is cut off at "Fly 15 km in t...". That's a lot of reading. "Fly again" button is big and clear - good. "Or fold and open to fly again" - handy.

**17 Home again.** Now there are more buttons: Daily Flight, Goals, Route (New), Birds. "Birds - Gull" but I was just told I got a Tern? Puzzled. "Everyone's sea - one try" - I don't know what that means.

**18 Route.** A map of lighthouses: Gull Point, Saltlight ticked, Kestrel Head next at 15 km. Quite nice, like a journey. But it reads right-to-left (Start is on the right), which feels backwards. And "...and on, as far as you can fly." is printed on top of "30 km" under Wren Ness - the words are jumbled together.

## My answers

1. **Hands:** Hold the phone open like a book. Close it (fold) as the bird goes down a wave, keep it closed to the bottom, then open it to fly up. Once per wave, don't flap.
2. **The storm:** A big rain cloud coming from behind. If it catches you, the go is over. Good landings ("Perfects") push it back.
3. **Where am I going:** Along the sea from lighthouse to lighthouse - Gull Point, Saltlight, then Kestrel Head next.
4. **Win or lose:** You lose when the storm catches you. You "win" a bit each time you reach a lighthouse. I'm not sure there's ever a proper end - "as far as you can fly".
