# Swoop: Tuck & Soar - first session (tester 15, iPhone)

24, casual mobile gamer (Subway Surfers, Crossy Road, Alto's Adventure), normal iPhone 18 Pro, just paid £1.99.

## Screen by screen

- **s1-01 (home)**: Pretty sunset, very Alto's. It's clear enough: "Hold anywhere. The wings fold." and a big yellow Fly button with "Next: Gull Point · 4 km". But there are two gulls, a big top-down one in the sky and a small side-on one on the wave, and I can't tell which one is me. The grey second line ("Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses.") is low contrast over the sea, and the small gull is drawn right on top of "lighthouses.". The two round icons bottom-left (gear, numbered list) have no labels.
- **s1-02 (tutorial, step 1 of 5)**: No text at all. There's a small landscape-phone icon with a circle in it, the storm raining on the left, and a thin grey line that runs ahead and dips *under* the water, so I don't know if it's a path or a glitch. For a second I thought it had frozen.
- **s1-03**: "Hold anywhere" turns up, but it sits under the gull's wing, so the bird covers part of "anywhere". A bit sloppy for a first instruction.
- **s1-04**: I hold, the icon goes yellow and the big top-down gull folds into a dart. That's a nice touch, and now I get it: the top bird shows my wings and the bottom bird is me. The game should tell me that, not leave me to work it out.
- **s1-05 (step 2)**: "Hold" with a gold glowing downslope and chevrons. I read it as "hold while going downhill", which is fine. Some things I don't understand: a yellow open-book or flag shape stuck on the gull's back, and a round bird button bottom-left with no label. Can I tap it? The top-down wing gull has gone.
- **s1-06**: "Let go too soon". Good, honest feedback, but I was never told *when* to let go, so I failed a rule I hadn't been given.
- **s1-07 (step 3)**: "Let go" at the bottom of the trough, with a gold ghost-bird outline on the next crest. That's clear and nice: let go at the bottom and fly to the ghost.
- **s1-08**: "Land here" floats over empty sea on the right with no marker, arrow or glow on the wave. Land *where*? And how? Is it another hold, or do I do nothing? This was my biggest "huh?".
- **s1-09**: "Perfect" in big gold with a gold ring. It feels good. The ring is drawn over the step dots at the bottom, so the two collide.
- **s1-10 (step 4)**: "One hold per wave". This is the most useful line in the whole tutorial: no tapping, one hold per wave. It should have come first. The bird button bottom-left has vanished in this frame and comes back in the next, which is inconsistent.
- **s1-11 (step 5)**: Again no text, I'm just gliding. A dead frame.
- **s1-12**: The storm bar appears top-left (storm icon, red-to-gold fill, my bird marker, then a long grey empty part) with "Stay ahead". "Gull Point" shows top-right over a lighthouse that stands on a big flat translucent rectangle, which looks like an unfinished placeholder block, not a rock. The "Gull Point" label also sits on the sun's glare. I'm guessing the grey part of the bar is the distance to the lighthouse, but nothing says so.
- **s1-13**: "Land a Perfect". OK, but why? It suggests Perfects push the storm back, but it doesn't say that.
- **s1-13b**: I land a Perfect, and as far as I can see the storm bar doesn't move. My marker is in the same place as in s1-12. So I can't see what my Perfect bought me. Then it's just "Stay ahead" again.
- **s1-14 (countdown)**: A big "2" and a caption, "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses....". That's four dots, which looks like truncated text or a typo. The faint bird button is back bottom-left over the rain.
- **s1-15 (run)**: A clean HUD at last: "60 m", "NEXT Gull Point · 4.0 km", and the storm bar now labelled "Storm". The dive looks good. This is the screen where it feels like a real game.
- **s1-15b (caught)**: "Flight over" at 12.8 km. The storm swallowed me, but the game never says "The storm caught you". The HUD has disappeared. The storm cloud is cut off by a hard vertical edge (around the middle of the screen), so it looks like a rectangle, and there are grey horizontal streak lines on the left. It looks broken.
- **s1-15c**: Same "Flight over" pill. The grey storm tint ends in a hard vertical line near the right edge, a clear rendering seam. The pill is flat and plain compared with the rest of the art.
- **s1-16 (results)**: Rewarding: "New bird: Tern, Level 2", "You made it past Gull Point and Saltlight!", 12.8 km, "Kestrel Head is 2.2 km further." Good hook. Problems: the "NEW LIGHTHOUSE" badge is singular when it just said I passed two; there's no reason given for ending; and there's no count of the Perfects I landed, even though a goal (s1-19) counted them.
- **s1-17 (home again)**: It's filled out now with Daily Flight · 27 Oct ("Same waves for everyone · 1 try"), Goals, Route and Birds (New). That makes it feel like a fuller product. The "Hold anywhere" instruction has gone from home. The two unlabeled round icons are still there, and the numbered-list one could be confused with Goals.
- **s1-18 (Route)**: The best screen in the game. A wavy map with checked lighthouses, me between Saltlight and Kestrel Head, upcoming ones, and "and on...". Now I understand where I'm going. I only found it after dying, though.
- **s1-19 (Goals)**: "Level 2 of 15 · next: New trail: Coral", "2 of 3 goals done". The third goal is below the fold, and the Level 5 card is cut off at the bottom. Levels 4 and 5 are so dimmed they're hard to read. There's a black pill on the left edge, which I assume is the Dynamic Island; it doesn't cover anything, but it only showed in this screen. Rank names ("Fledgling", "Puddle Hopper", "Spray Chaser") are cute. "Golden Hour" in Level 1 is never explained (a sky, I assume).

**Lesser-version feeling:** Nothing told me I was missing a foldable feature, and "Hold anywhere" works fine on a normal phone, so I didn't feel short-changed. The only odd bits are the yellow open-book shape on the gull's back (s1-05/06) and the folding top-down gull, which look like they're meant for something I don't have. They aren't explained, so they come across as decoration I don't understand.

## The four questions

1. **What do my fingers do?** Put one finger anywhere and hold while the gull goes down a wave, let go at the bottom to launch up, then (I think) hold again to land on the next downslope. No repeated tapping: "One hold per wave" (s1-10) says so plainly. What I'm still unsure about is the landing: "Land here" (s1-08) never told me what to do with my finger.
2. **What is the storm?** A dark raincloud chasing me from the left. The bar at the top shows how close it is. If it catches me, the flight is over (s1-15b). I *think* Perfect landings push it back (s1-13), but I never saw the bar react.
3. **Where am I going?** Along a coast of lighthouses: Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9 km, Kestrel Head 15 km, Old Harry 22 km, Wren Ness 30 km, "and on...". It's an endless distance run with checkpoints. That only fully clicked on the Route screen (s1-18).
4. **What is success and failure?** Success: distance, passing lighthouses, Perfect landings, finishing goals to level up and unlock birds, trails and skies. Failure: the storm catches you and it says "Flight over". Letting go too soon is a mistake that costs speed, not a death, as far as I can tell.

## Verdicts

- **Finished and premium?** Mostly. The art direction, the results card, the Route map and the Goals ladder feel like a proper £1.99 game. The things that feel unfinished: the hard rectangular edges on the storm (s1-15b, s1-15c), the translucent block under the lighthouse (s1-12), text colliding with sprites (s1-01, s1-03, s1-09), the "lighthouses...." caption (s1-14), and two tutorial frames with no text (s1-02, s1-11). Polished in the menus, rough at the seams in-game.
- **Worth £1.99?** Just about, yes. It looks better than most free games, and the Daily Flight plus unlocks give it legs. It isn't yet at the Alto's level it's clearly aiming for.
- **Would I keep playing?** A few more runs, yes. "Kestrel Head is 2.2 km further" is a good hook, and the Tern unlock made me want to try it. Whether I'm still playing in a week depends on whether I ever understand how to beat the storm.
- **Single most confusing moment:** s1-08, "Land here" floating over empty water with no target and no instruction for my finger.

**Top 5 fixes (most important first):**
1. **s1-15b / s1-15c / s1-16: say why I died and fix the storm edges.** Replace "Flight over" with "The storm caught you" (with the km) and repeat it on the results card. Remove the hard vertical seams and the rectangular cut-off on the storm cloud and tint; they're the most broken-looking thing in the session.
2. **s1-08 (and s1-05/06): make the landing and release rules explicit.** Put a visible target on the wave for "Land here" and say what to do ("Hold again as you come down"). On s1-05, change "Hold" to something like "Hold down the slope, let go at the bottom", so "Let go too soon" in s1-06 isn't a surprise. Move "One hold per wave" (s1-10) to the start.
3. **s1-12 / s1-13 / s1-13b: show what the storm bar means and make Perfects visibly push it back.** Label the bar ends (storm / you / lighthouse) and make the gap jump on a Perfect with a "+ gap" pop. Right now "Land a Perfect" gives no visible reward. Replace the flat translucent block under the Gull Point lighthouse with a real rock.
4. **s1-01 / s1-03 / s1-09 / s1-14: stop text and sprites colliding.** Gull over "lighthouses." on home, wing over "Hold anywhere", Perfect ring over the step dots, and the four-dot "lighthouses...." caption. These small things make it look cheap.
5. **s1-05 / s1-02 / s1-01 / s1-16: explain or remove the mystery UI.** The yellow book/flag on the gull's back; the unlabeled bird button bottom-left that comes and goes (s1-05, s1-10, s1-14); which bird is me versus the wing indicator (s1-02); labels for the two round home icons; and fix "NEW LIGHTHOUSE" to plural when I passed two.

## Scores

- Understood: **7/10**
- Feels finished: **6/10**
- Keep playing: **6/10**
