## Plain answers

1. **Hands:** Hold the phone open like a book. When the bird goes down a wave, fold it a bit (a "tuck") and keep it folded all the way to the bottom. At the bottom, open it up and the bird flies up. Repeat for every wave.
2. **The storm:** A dark rain cloud chasing me from behind. If it reaches the bird, the flight is over. Reaching a lighthouse pushes it back a bit.
3. **Where I'm going:** Along a line of lighthouses. First Gull Point at 4 km, then Saltlight at 9 km, then more, "as far as you can fly".
4. **Win/lose:** You lose when the storm catches you. There's no real "win". You just try to pass more lighthouses and go further than last time. "Perfects" apparently help, but I never learned what a Perfect is.
