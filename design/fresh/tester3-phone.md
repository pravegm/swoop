# Swoop: Tuck & Soar, first launch on a normal iPhone 17 (tester 3)

22, I play Alto's Odyssey, Tiny Wings, Geometry Dash and Vampire Survivors. Paid £1.99 because a friend mentioned it. I know nothing else about it.

4. **2/7 again.** Now it says "Press and hold anywhere..." So it's hold after all. It changed its mind in front of me.
5. **3/7.** Side view now, the gull on a wave. "Hold the screen all the way down the wave." There's a "Faster!" bar. OK, this is Tiny Wings: hold on the downhill.

## The four questions
1. **Finger:** press and hold while the bird is going down a wave, and let go at the bottom so it launches off the upslope. Then hold again so it lands on the next downslope ("Perfect"). One hold per wave, no tapping.
2. **Storm:** the thing chasing me from the left. The bar shows how close it is. Perfects and tailwinds push it back, and so does reaching a lighthouse.
3. **Where:** along a route of lighthouses (Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9 km, Kestrel Head 15 km...), with no end.
4. **Success/failure:** you fail when the storm catches you. Success is how far you get: lighthouses passed, new bests, goals ticked, ranks and new birds.

## Vs Tiny Wings and Alto
- The core is Tiny Wings: hold on the downhill, release up, land on a downslope. The waves look great, but it's basically the same verb. The storm chasing you is Tiny Wings' sunset timer turned round, and the lighthouses are its islands.
- The look and the goals/rank/daily setup are very Alto. That's a compliment, though it isn't as polished as Alto yet.
- **Does it feel lesser?** A bit, at first. In the tutorial I thought "Tiny Wings on water". The storm bar, the lighthouse targets and the route map won me back. Having a named next stop is a clearer goal than Tiny Wings gives you. The UI bugs make it look cheaper than Alto.

## The "made for iPhone Duo" line
- On the very first screen it tells me I'm on the lesser version ("Here, hold to tuck"). That puts me off a little. I paid £1.99 and it's telling me the real game is on another phone.
- The top-down gull with the white wingtip dots, and "These are your wings", obviously mean something on a Duo. On my phone they're just confusing decoration.
- I don't feel cheated once I'm playing, because the hold control works fine. But the game keeps reminding me, so I end up wondering what I'm missing. I'd just not mention it on a normal iPhone.

## What's confusing
- Screens 1, 3, 4, 8: hold the bird, then tap, then hold anywhere, then "no tapping". The tutorial contradicts itself.
- Screens 8 and 9: the step order jumps (6, then 5), and step 4 never appears.
- Screen 2: "turn sideways" when I'm already sideways, and the drawing doesn't look like a phone.
- Screen 12: a blank navy screen in portrait. It looks like a crash.
- Screens 13, 17, 25: text stacked on other text, or cards with stuff showing through.
- Screen 15: "Too close" in red at 61 m is alarming. "YOUR GOAL" under the distance reads like the distance is the goal. I don't know what the red tick on the storm bar means, or what the empty track to my right is.
- Screens 20 and 22: a goal ticked while showing "11 of 25", and "159 of 25" overflowing.
- Screens 20–23: "previous best 6.42 km" never updates, even after 7.70 and 12.83.
- Screens 22–24: cut-off names, and broken home tiles ("...", the letters of "New" stacked).

## What's great
- The art: the sunset, the waves, the splashes and the tailwind streak are gorgeous.
- The fail messages in school (7, 10) tell you exactly what you did wrong.
- The storm-versus-you bar is a simple, tense reason to keep going.
- Named lighthouses plus the route map (23) give me "one more go to reach Saltlight". That's a great hook.
- Tailwind chains (16) give skilled players something to chase.
- The results screen tip (21) is genuinely helpful.

## Scores
- **I got it: 7/10.** By the first real run I knew what to do, but the tutorial's mixed tap/hold messages and the glitches slowed me down.
- **I'd keep playing: 6/10.** It feels great and the lighthouse hook works. It needs to feel less like Tiny Wings on water, and the bugs need fixing before I'd show it to my friend.
