# Swoop: Tuck & Soar - first session (tester 16, iPhone Duo)

Me: 33, nurse, casual player (Alto's Odyssey, Two Dots, Wordle). I paid £1.99 and I'm holding the Duo open like a book.

## Screen by screen

- **s1-01 home**: Nice-looking title screen. "Hold it like a book. These are your wings." plus a big top-down gull across both halves tells me straight away that the two halves are the wings. "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses." gives me a goal. Two things confused me. First, there are two birds: the big top-down gull, and a small side-on gull skimming the wave at bottom right, so I'm not sure which one is "me". Second, the grey subtitle line ("Stay ahead...") is low-contrast where it sits over the storm cloud.
- **s1-02 school-hold**: "Hold it open" with a little hinge icon. Clear enough. The tutorial puts the instruction text on top of the dark rain cloud, so it reads fine, but the scene is busy. The big top-down gull is still filling the top of the screen while a second small gull sits on the wave. I still don't know which one I'm steering.
- **s1-03 school-wings**: "Fold a little / One fold per wave". The dashed guide shows a shallow V. "One fold per wave" is the first hint that I don't flap over and over. Good. But it comes before I know what a "wave" means for timing.
- **s1-04 school-wings-done**: Tick, and the top gull tucks its wings into a dart. That's satisfying, a good moment. But the hinge icon shows quite a deep V, much more than "a little". That's a mild contradiction: how far am I meant to fold?
- **s1-05 school-dive**: Scene switches to daytime. "Fold down the wave / Open at the bottom". Left-pointing chevrons on the wave crest show the direction. Clear-ish. The top-down gull has vanished with no comment, which answers my earlier question by accident.
- **s1-06 school-dive-fail**: "Opened too soon / Stay folded to the bottom". Useful feedback. But the bird is in exactly the same spot on the crest as in s1-05, so it looks like nothing happened. Was I punished before I'd even started down?
- **s1-07 school-soar**: "Open", with a flat hinge icon, as the bird climbs the next slope. Fine. But there's a **yellow ghost-bird outline clipped off the left edge** with a dotted trail. It's never explained. Is it a target? A rival? It looks like a stray asset.
- **s1-08 school-land**: "Land here / Fold to come down", with a gold ring at bottom left and a gold ring around my bird. Three problems. (1) The target ring is **clipped by the left edge and sits right on top of the Skip button**. (2) The sun-glint dashes on the sea run **through the "Land here" text**. (3) The bird already looks like it's sitting on the water, so "fold to come down" makes no sense to me. Which way is down from the water?
- **s1-09 school-perfect**: Big gold "Perfect", and the bird hit a ring. Feels good. But now there are two gold rings on screen (one round my bird, one ahead with a drop arrow) and I can't tell which one I just "landed in".
- **s1-10 school-rhythm**: "One fold per wave". It repeats the rule, which is good, but it's floating text on an empty sky with no visual of the rhythm (fold, open, fold, open).
- **s1-11 school-storm-1**: A storm bar appears at the top: my bird icon in the middle, a storm icon at the right end. "Stay ahead". I worked out that the storm chases me from behind (right), since I fly left. The bar reads as a gap meter, not a progress bar, which took a second to get.
- **s1-12 school-storm-2**: The Gull Point lighthouse appears top left. Nice. "Land in the gold ring", but **there is no gold ring anywhere on screen**. A dotted line runs off the **right** edge (behind me), so the ring is either off-screen or behind me. Confusing.
- **s1-13 school-storm-3**: Identical to s1-12, still with no ring visible. It looked stuck, and I'd have wondered if the game had frozen.
- **s1-13b school-storm-push**: "Perfect / Perfect pushes it back". The bird icon on the bar jumps left with a "+". The storm cloud slides almost off-screen. This is the clearest teaching moment in the game. "It" means the storm, which I only get from context.
- **s1-14 run-countdown**: "2 / Wings open, hold steady". Clear. The storm bar has **lost its "Storm" label** here, and the storm position has reset from where I'd pushed it in the tutorial. That's slightly deflating after "Perfect pushes it back".
- **s1-15 run**: HUD reads 66 m, "NEXT Gull Point · 4 km". The gull dives with a lovely trail. The HUD is clean. 4 km feels like a very long way when the counter is in metres.
- **s1-15b caught**: "The storm caught you", 12.8 km, and the bar icons touch. Clear failure. The pause button has gone, which is fine. I never saw a lighthouse go by on the way, so I don't know if anything marked passing Gull Point or Saltlight.
- **s1-15c flight-over**: Same headline again, as the storm rolls over. A nice moody shot, but it's a second "caught you" screen before results, which drags.
- **s1-16 results**: "YOUR FIRST FLIGHT / You made it past Gull Point and Saltlight! / 2 NEW LIGHTHOUSES / 12.8 km / The storm caught you 2.2 km before Kestrel Head." Good framing: a loss that feels like progress. "Or fold and open to fly again" is a nice touch. But the right card says **"New bird: Tern / Level 2 reward"** and I've never been told about levels. Why did I level up? The card is also mostly empty dark space above and below. And what does "Choose Tern" change? Is it just how it looks, or how it plays?
- **s1-17 home**: Now there's Daily Flight, Goals, Route and Birds (Tern, with a dot). The tagline has dropped the "hold it like a book" line. Fine for a return visit. The **small gull and its splash are partly hidden behind the Scores button**. "Fly · Next: Kestrel Head · 15 km" is a good hook.
- **s1-18 route**: A lovely idea: a coastline with lighthouses at 4, 9, 15, 22 and 30 km, "and on...". It reads **right-to-left**, which matches the flight. Problems: (1) The solid gold "travelled" line **stops at Saltlight (9 km)**, but the bird marker sits further on at about 12.8 km, so they disagree. (2) The **bird marker faces right**, back toward the start, when I fly left. (3) The storm icon at the far right is **clipped by the screen edge**. (4) The bottom two-thirds of the screen is empty navy.
- **s1-19 goals**: "Level 2 of 15 · next: Coral trail", 2 of 3 goals done. Clear checklist and a sensible unlock ladder. Problems: (1) The **Level 8 row is cut off at the bottom** (its subtitle is half clipped). (2) There's a **faint bird shape bleeding through behind the Level 5 row** (around the "Puffin" line). It looks like background art showing through the card. (3) Level names like "Fledgling" and "Puddle Hopper" sit under the rewards, so it's unclear whether that's my title or the level's name.

## The four questions

1. **What do my hands do (and do I flap repeatedly or not)?** I hold the Duo open flat like a book, and the two halves are the wings. Folding the halves toward each other tucks the wings and dives the bird down the front of a wave. Opening them back out at the bottom of the wave makes it soar up the next one. I fold once more to come down into the gold ring. **No repeated flapping**: it's "one fold per wave", a slow rhythm of fold to dive and open to climb. I'm not sure how deep the fold should be ("a little" in s1-03 versus a deep V in s1-04).
2. **What is the storm?** A storm cloud chasing me from behind (from the right). The bar at the top shows the gap between me and it. Perfect landings push it back. If it reaches me, the flight ends.
3. **Where am I going?** Left along the coast, from lighthouse to lighthouse: Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9 km, Kestrel Head 15 km, and so on. It's an endless run with named checkpoints.
4. **What is success and failure?** Success means flying far, passing lighthouses, and getting "Perfect" ring landings that push the storm back, plus completing goals that unlock birds, skies and trails. Failure means the storm catches me ("The storm caught you"). Dive timing mistakes like "Opened too soon" seem to cost speed rather than end the run.

## Verdicts

- **Finished and premium?** Mostly. The art direction, sky gradients, results framing and route map feel like a £1.99 game. But clipped elements (the s1-08 ring, the s1-18 storm icon, the s1-19 Level 8 row), the unexplained ghost bird (s1-07), the art bleeding through (s1-19) and the "gold ring" that isn't there (s1-12/13) chip at that. It's about 80% polished.
- **Would you keep playing?** Probably for a few more runs. "2.2 km before Kestrel Head" and the Daily Flight are good hooks. Whether I stay depends on whether the fold feels good in the hand, and the screens can't tell me that.
- **Single most confusing moment:** s1-12/s1-13: "Land in the gold ring" with no gold ring visible and the dotted line running off-screen behind me. It's a close second to s1-08's "Fold to come down" when the bird already looks like it's on the water.
- **Anything that looks broken?** The ghost bird clipped at the left edge (s1-07). The target ring clipped and colliding with Skip (s1-08). No ring at all (s1-12, s1-13). The route's travelled line not reaching the bird marker, and the marker facing the wrong way (s1-18). Level 8 cut off and art bleeding behind Level 5 (s1-19). The storm label missing (s1-14).

**Top 5 things to fix (most important first):**
1. **s1-12 / s1-13**: Show the gold ring on screen when you tell me to land in it, ahead of the bird and not off the right edge behind it. As it stands it looks frozen or broken.
2. **s1-08**: Move the "Land here" ring fully on-screen and away from the Skip button. Keep the sea-glint dashes out from under the text. Make it visually obvious the bird is *above* the water before "Fold to come down", or reword it.
3. **s1-07**: Explain or remove the yellow ghost bird clipped at the left edge. If it's a target or rival, introduce it. If it's a leftover asset, kill it.
4. **s1-16 / s1-19**: Introduce levels and goals before rewarding them. "Level 2 reward" out of nowhere is confusing. Also fix the clipped Level 8 row and the art showing through behind Level 5 in s1-19. Say whether a new bird changes how it plays or only how it looks.
5. **s1-18**: Make the gold travelled line reach my 12.8 km bird marker. Point the marker the way I fly (left). Un-clip the storm icon, and use the empty lower two-thirds of the screen (best run, recent runs, or the next lighthouse's reward).

Honourable mentions: settle "fold a little" versus the deep V (s1-03/s1-04); keep the "Storm" label on the bar (s1-14); stop the home-screen gull hiding behind the Scores button (s1-17); clarify which bird is me (s1-01/s1-02).
