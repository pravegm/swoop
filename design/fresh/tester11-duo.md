# Tester 11: pharmacist, 47, plays Wordle and nothing else. iPhone Duo, first session of "Swoop"

## Screen by screen

1. **Home.** It's pretty: a big seagull, a sunset, a storm cloud on the right. "Hold it like a book. These are your wings." Fine, I understand that. The two halves of the phone are the wings. I press the big yellow "Fly". I don't know what "Gull Point · 4 km" is yet. Maybe a place?
2. **"Hold it open."** There's a drawing of an open book, so I hold the phone open. The big gull at the top has its wings spread. Easy.
3. **"Fold a little."** The drawing looks exactly like the last one, so I couldn't tell from the picture how far to close it. I closed it a bit.
4. **The wings tuck in.** The gull at the top folds its wings into an arrow and the book drawing goes beige. So closing the phone tucks the wings. That's quite nice. It said nothing, but I guessed I'd done it right.
5. **"Fold."** A daytime sea now, with a small gull on a wave and a tiny yellow book on its back. I think it wants me to close the phone as the bird goes down the slope.
6. **"Stay folded."** The book is now a dashed outline. Did I open it too early? Nothing told me I'd done anything wrong, so I just kept it shut.
7. **"Open."** The bird is going downhill with a yellow trail behind it. There's also a faint golden outline of a bird further back. Is that me? A ghost? I opened the phone.
8. **"Land here."** There are rings on the water next to the bird. I suppose I should touch down there. I didn't do anything different with my hands. I just waited.
9. **No text.** The bird skims along with lots of splash. I only learned later that this was a "Perfect". Nothing on the screen said so.
10. **"One per wave."** One what? Fold? Landing? There's a small blue book with three up-arrows, yellow arrowheads on the wave, dotted lines and the ghost bird again. This was the busiest screen and the one I understood least.
11. **"Stay ahead."** Ahead of what? There's nothing behind me.
12-13. **A bar appears at the top**, with a bird at one end and a cloud at the other, plus a lighthouse labelled "Gull Point". Now I guess the cloud is chasing me and the lighthouse is where I'm going. The bar has no words on it.
14. **Countdown "2"**, "Wings open, hold steady". Clear. The real game is starting.
15. **"64 m"**, "NEXT Gull Point · 4.0 km", with a "<" arrow. The bar now says "Storm". I fold going down and open going up. The bird flies to the left, which felt backwards to me, like reading right to left.
15b. **"Flight over" at 12.8 km.** The rain cloud is right on top of me, so I think the storm caught me. It didn't actually say that.
16. **Results:** "You made it past Saltlight!", "New lighthouse", "12.8 km". This is the screen that finally explains it: "Perfect landings push the storm back." I wish I'd been told that before I flew. There's also "New bird: Tern, Level 2" and "Or fold and open to fly again". Two boxes at once was a lot to take in.
17. **Home again**, and it says more now: "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses." That's the clearest sentence in the game and it only shows up after my first go. There's also "Daily Flight · 27 Oct, Same waves for everyone · 1 try", which is like Wordle, and I like that. There are buttons for Goals, Route and Birds ("New", "Gull").
18. **Route.** A coast with lighthouses: Gull Point 4 ✓, Saltlight 9 ✓, Kestrel Head 15 (Next), Old Harry, Wren Ness, Lantern Rock. Very clear, like a map. There's a cloud and a pier at the start.
19. **Goals.** "Level 2 of 15 · Puddle Hopper · 2 of 3 done". That makes sense: fly 3 km, "3 Perfects", fly 15 km in total. The right-hand list lost me: "Fledgling · Gull · Golden Hour · Natural", "Spray Chaser · Coral", "Next rank: Coral". Is Coral a bird, a colour or a rank? Level, rank and title all seem to mean the same thing.

## My four answers
1. **Hands:** Hold the phone open like a book. Close it partway when the bird goes down a wave, which tucks the wings and makes it dive. Open it again at the bottom or going up so it glides, and try to come down on the next wave where the rings are. I think it's one fold per wave.
2. **The storm:** The dark rain cloud behind me. The bar at the top shows how close it is. Good landings push it back.
3. **Where I'm going:** Along the coast from one lighthouse to the next (Gull Point, Saltlight, Kestrel Head and so on). Each one is further away.
4. **Win or lose:** I lose when the storm catches me ("Flight over"). There's no real "win". I get further, pass lighthouses, tick off goals and unlock birds.
