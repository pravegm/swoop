# Tester 1 (Duo): first launch of "Swoop: Tuck & Soar"

I'm 34 and I play Wordle, Candy Crush and a bit of Crossy Road. I paid £1.99 and opened it on my new Duo. These notes cover only what I saw.

## Screen by screen

1. **Home.** A big gull with its wings across both halves, a sunset, and a storm cloud on the right. "Hold your Duo like a book. These are your wings." Lovely, and I get it straight away: the two halves are the wings. Fly says "Next: Gull Point · 4 km". I tap Fly.
2. **Flight School 1 of 8.** "Hold your Duo open like a book." A little phone picture and a fold/open dial. Easy, I just hold it.
3. **Same step.** It adds "A little less than flat." OK, I relax the hinge a bit.
4. **Step 2.** "These are your wings. Fold a little..." I close it slightly.
5. **"That's a tuck."** A tick. So a slight fold is called a tuck. Good, I've learned a word.
6. **"One quick check."** Two cards, one per half. It says the Duo "softens one half of the screen" when it starts to fold. I didn't know that, and it felt like a hardware test rather than a game. I open it flat as told.
7. **"...now fold it a little."** I fold.
8. **"Which side stayed sharp?"** Stripes over the logo, a left/right choice. I had to actually look, and I wasn't sure. I'd guess, or tap "Not sure · skip".
9. **"Got it. Your bird flies on the sharp side."** Fine, but I'd half-forgotten why I was doing it.
10. **Step 4, now in a game scene.** "Hold the fold all the way down the wave." The bird sits on a wave crest with a "Faster!" bar. I fold and hold.
11. **Same.** I keep holding.
12. **"You opened too soon. Keep it folded to the bottom."** Clear, kind feedback. I try again.
13. **Step 5.** "Open at the bottom... and fly!" / "Open now!" I open and the bird shoots up the next wave with spray. This is the core move: tuck going down, open at the bottom.
14. **Step 8 of 8.** "Reach the first lighthouse: Gull Point · 4 km." It jumped from step 5 to step 8, so I never saw steps 6 and 7. A bar appears at the top with my bird and a storm icon.
15. **Running at 43 m.** "Storm behind you. Don't let it catch you." The bar shows "You", "Storm" and "Too close" in red. Is the storm close right now? I'm in the yellow part, not the red, so I think I'm OK?
16. **245 m.** Same message, and the cloud is right behind me on screen. "Too close" is still showing. Worrying, but nothing happens.
17. **156 m.** (This one has a lower number than the last. Maybe it's a different try.) Same message.
18. **315 m.** The labels are gone and my marker has slid left, away from the storm. I think that's good.
19. **Countdown "2".** "Wings open, hold steady." So I keep it open before the start. Clear.
20. **929 m.** "Tailwind! 3 Perfects in a row = a burst of speed." Wait, what's a Perfect? Nobody told me. I suppose it's a well-timed tuck and open. The chip says x4 TAILWIND.
21. **1.45 km.** "Perfect x5" in gold with a ring round the bird. OK, I'm getting Perfects, but I don't know exactly what I did right.
22. **3.75 km.** A lighthouse appears on the left. "Gull Point · 250 m". Exciting, my target is in sight. x9 tailwind.
23. **"Gull Point reached! Lighthouse 1 · the storm falls back."** Great payoff. The next target is "Saltlight · 4.89 km". Lighthouses push the storm back.
24. **2.48 km, another flight.** My marker is jammed against the storm end and the bar is outlined red. The cloud is overhead and it's raining on me. Clearly bad.
25. **"The storm caught you."** That's the fail. Understood.
26. **Results.** "Reached Cormorant Stack! … 92.78 km. NEW BEST! Previous best 6.42 km", "12 lighthouses", "159 Perfects · chain 32". The goals list ticks "Done in an earlier flight". 92 km?! I'm confused about which flight this is.
27. **Results, new route.** The header now says "FIRST FLIGHT" but shows the same 92.78 km. It says "+2 ranks", then "New goals: Reach the first lighthouse in one flight", which I've just done 12 times. The Daily Flight is "one try". It doesn't add up.
28. **Route map.** Lighthouses along a coast, with the bird flying leftward: Gull Point 4 km (ticked), Saltlight 9 km "Next", Kestrel Head 15, then Lighthouse 4 and 5 and "...as far as you can fly". This is the clearest "where am I going" screen. But it says "1 lighthouse reached, Best 6.42 km", which contradicts the 92 km and the 12 lighthouses.
29. **Home, folded.** The gull has its wings tucked, which is cute and mirrors my hinge. But it asks the "Which half stays sharp?" check again (didn't I do that?). The bottom tiles are squashed into "G...", "Ro...", "Bi..." with "New" stacked one letter per line. It looks broken.
30. **Paused.** "Outfly the storm and reach the next lighthouse." This is the best one-line explanation in the game, and it's hidden in the pause menu.
31. **Closed the phone mid-run (cover screen).** "Open your wings to keep flying. Storm 254 m behind. Beat 6.42 km to set a best." Clear and clever. Closing it is a pause, and opening it carries on.

## The four questions

1. **Hands.** Hold it open like a book, a bit less than flat: those are the wings. When the bird goes down a wave, fold it a little (a "tuck") and hold that all the way to the bottom. At the bottom, open it to launch up and over the next wave. Then repeat for every wave. It's a rhythm, one tuck and open per wave, not fast flapping. Hold it open and steady during the countdown. Closing it fully pauses the game.
2. **Storm.** A dark rain cloud chasing me from behind (the right side). The bar at the top shows how close it is. Going fast, chaining Perfects and reaching lighthouses push it back.
3. **Where.** Along a coast from lighthouse to lighthouse: Gull Point (4 km), Saltlight (9 km), Kestrel Head (15 km), and on with no end. I'm aiming for the next lighthouse and a new best distance.
4. **Success and failure.** Success is reaching lighthouses, getting Perfects in a row (tailwind) and beating my best km. Failure is the storm catching me, which ends the flight. There's also a small fail in training: opening too soon.

## Three most confusing moments

1. **The storm bar (15–18, 24).** "Too close" sits in red text the whole time, even when I'm far away in the yellow. I couldn't tell whether the yellow strip was my safety gap or a danger zone. I only understood it at 24, when it went fully red.
2. **"Perfect" was never taught (14, 20, 21).** The tutorial jumped from step 5 to step 8. Then "3 Perfects in a row" pops up with no explanation of what a Perfect is or what timing earns one. I was getting them without knowing why.
3. **Results don't agree with each other (26–28, 29).** It says 92.78 km and 12 lighthouses and calls it "FIRST FLIGHT". The route says 1 lighthouse and best 6.42 km. The new goal is "reach the first lighthouse". Then home asks the sharp-side check again, and the bottom tiles are squashed to "G..." with "N/e/w" stacked. I stopped trusting the numbers.

Honourable mention: the sharp-side check (6–9) felt like a phone diagnostic in the middle of a fun intro.

## What delighted me

- The phone really is the wings. The home gull spreads out across both halves, and when I fold, the gull tucks too (29). It made me smile.
- Tuck down the wave, open at the bottom, and whoosh: spray and a speed trail. It feels physical.
- "You opened too soon. Keep it folded to the bottom." That's exactly the help I needed.
- The lighthouse popping up on the horizon, and "Gull Point reached! the storm falls back."
- Closing the phone pauses it with a calm "Open your wings to keep flying" (31). Very clever.
- The sunset art is gorgeous.

## Would I keep playing?

Yes, for a while. The fold-to-dive rhythm is new and satisfying, and "just get to the next lighthouse" pulls me on. I'd stop if the numbers keep contradicting each other or I never work out how to get Perfects on purpose.

## "I understood it" score

**7 / 10.** The core move and the goal came through. The storm meter, Perfects and the results screens did not.
