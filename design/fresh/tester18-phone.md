# Swoop: Tuck & Soar - first session (tester 18, iPhone)

29, agency UI designer; Monument Valley / Alto's Odyssey / Mini Metro fan; judges against Apple Design Award winners; plays on a regular iPhone 18 Pro; paid £1.99.

## Screen by screen

- **s1-01 home** - Asks me to tap Fly ("Next: Gull Point · 4 km"). The sunset palette is lovely. The layout is a weak left/right split: the title block hugs the left, the Fly card floats on the right, and there's a dead zone in the middle. The Fly card's top doesn't line up with the "Swoop" cap height or with anything else. The Settings/Scores pills sit right on the wave crest, so the wave line runs through the Scores pill. There are three text tiers under the title (TUCK & SOAR, bold white "Hold anywhere. The wings fold.", muted "Stay ahead…") and they fight each other. "lighthouses." is orphaned on its own line and nearly touches the small gull sprite and spray. The big top-down spread-wing bird at the top does nothing I can see. It's an ornament with no job.
- **s1-02 school-hold** - Step 1/5: "Hold it sideways". I'm already holding it sideways, so the instruction makes no sense on my phone. It reads like it was written for a different device. The caption sits on the storm cloud edge and the rain streaks. The hand icon is a different size and style from the one in s1-05. The page dots and the Skip button sit low over the wave line. The spread-wing bird is back, still with no job.
- **s1-03 school-wings** - A solid navy **portrait** frame, completely blank. It's a dead frame in a landscape game. It looks like a rotation or orientation bug.
- **s1-04 school-wings-done** - The worst frame of the session. It's a rotated, half-composited mess: the greyed-out home screen (Swoop title, Settings, Scores) is ghosted over a tilted portrait gameplay scene, with black letterboxing on the right and a stray check mark and a "next" chevron floating in the middle. It looks like a snapshot of a broken transition. If this is a step I'm meant to do, I can't tell what it was or why it counted as done.
- **s1-05 school-dive** - Step 2: "Hold down the wave / Let go at the bottom". The message is clear and the yellow glowing slope plus chevrons are nice. But the "bottom" isn't on screen, because the slope runs off the bottom-right corner. The two-tier caption (white bold plus muted subline) is good. The dashed yellow hold ring is a third style of hand icon.
- **s1-06 school-dive-fail** - "Let go too soon / Keep holding to the bottom". It's almost pixel-identical to s1-05: the bird is in the same spot and only the chevrons moved. There's no visible consequence (no stall, no colour change, no bird reaction), so the failure has no feel. It's just a text swap.
- **s1-07 school-soar** - Step 3: "Let go". The hand icon shrank again (third size so far) and now sits in a thin cyan ring. The caption is now a single line with no subline, so the tier rule from the earlier steps is gone. There's a yellow shape clipped at the right edge (a ring or beam?) that's cut off.
- **s1-08 school-land** - "Land here / Hold to come down". **The gold landing ring is drawn directly under the Skip button.** The target I'm supposed to aim for is covered by a UI control. The dotted guide arrow ends on Skip too. The caption sits on top of the sun-glitter lines, which hurts contrast. I'm also still on dot 3, the same as "Let go", so the step count is off.
- **s1-09 school-perfect** - "Perfect" in big gold type with a ring around the bird. It feels good and it's the most rewarding moment so far. But "Perfect" is a heavy rounded display face while every other caption is a medium sans, so the type systems don't match. The pause button sits over the horizon band with no backing contrast.
- **s1-10 school-rhythm** - "One hold per wave". Clear and useful. The ring around the bird is now faint grey instead of gold, and nothing explains the difference. That's a lot of empty sky for one line of copy.
- **s1-11 school-storm-1** - Step 5: the storm meter appears ("Storm" icon, gradient bar, bird puck) with the caption "Stay ahead". The storm cloud sprite is clipped behind the pause button in the top-left corner. The bar has a labelled left end (Storm), but the right end has no marker or label, so I don't know what the bar is measuring toward. The dark brown empty track looks muddy against the sunset.
- **s1-12 school-storm-2** - "Land in the gold ring", **but there is no gold ring anywhere on screen.** The dotted yellow line starts at the left edge and stops under the caption, pointing at nothing. The "Gull Point" label is drawn straight through the lighthouse sprite, which pokes up between "Gull" and "Point". That's a plain collision.
- **s1-13 school-storm-3** - The same frame as s1-12 again: the ring is still missing and the lighthouse is still through the label. This feels like a wait state with nothing to do.
- **s1-13b school-storm-push** - "Perfect pushes it back" + "Perfect" + a "+" over the bar as the bird puck moves right. This finally clicks. But there are two stacked captions in two typefaces at two sizes, both left-aligned to different x positions ("Perfect pushes it back" starts at about x330, "Perfect" at about x370). We're on dot 5 of 5 for the fourth frame running.
- **s1-14 run-countdown** - A solid navy landscape frame, empty apart from the Dynamic Island pill. If this is the countdown, it has no numbers and no scene. Another dead frame right before my first real run.
- **s1-15 run** - First real flight. The HUD shows Storm bar, "60 m" and "NEXT · Gull Point · 4 km". After 60 m it still says 4 km, so I can't tell whether that's the remaining distance or the lighthouse's position. The "NEXT" tag butts into the top edge of the pill. The dive streak runs in behind the Dynamic Island. The bar still has no goal marker on the right.
- **s1-15b caught** - "The storm caught you" at 12.8 km. The headline is set right on top of the storm cloud texture, so the white type sits on mottled grey. The bar turns red and the puck slams to the left, which is good, readable feedback. The pause button vanishes and nothing replaces it, which leaves an awkward hole in the top-left. The HUD shows only "12.8 km", and the "NEXT" pill has disappeared.
- **s1-15c flight-over** - A desaturated, rainy hold on the failure. The mood is nice. The text is identical and in a slightly different position from s1-15b, so it jumps about 90px down between frames.
- **s1-16 results** - Two cards. Right card: "YOUR FIRST FLIGHT", a two-line yellow headline ("You made it past Gull Point and Saltlight!"), a "2 NEW LIGHTHOUSES" pill, a huge "12.8 km", and a subline. The yellow headline and the white 12.8 km compete to be the hero. Saltlight appears here for the first time, and I never saw it during the run. Left card: "New bird: Tern / Level 2 reward". This is the **first time levels exist**, and nothing tells me what a level is or how I got one. The top third of that card is empty and the content floats low and off-centre against the right card. The "Choose Tern" outline button style matches nothing else. The Home and Share buttons are icon-only with no labels.
- **s1-17 home (return)** - New things have appeared: Daily Flight, Goals, Route, Birds. Now there are **two navigation systems**: pill buttons (Settings, Scores) bottom-left and square tiles (Goals, Route, Birds) bottom-right. Scores and Goals sound like the same thing. The "Hold anywhere. The wings fold." line from s1-01 is gone, and the tagline has been promoted to large white text, so the type hierarchy changed between two visits to the same screen. There are sparkle particles sitting on top of the Settings pill. A gold ring and a gull are clipped by the bottom edge. The Birds tile's icon and label sit higher than Goals and Route. The Daily Flight calendar glyph is a fuzzy, low-detail icon.
- **s1-18 route** - The best screen in the app, and properly Mini Metro-ish. But the lighthouses use three illustration styles: red-and-white filled for passed, yellow outline at a bigger size for next, grey outline for future. The big yellow one sits low in its trough. "and on…" is jammed against the right edge with no lighthouse. My trail line stops at Saltlight while my bird icon sits further on, which is honest (12.8 km) but unlabelled. The Fly button is set inside a second dark capsule, a double container that looks like a leftover. The labels row has no visual tie (tick or stem) to the lighthouses above.
- **s1-19 goals** - "Level 2 of 15 · next: Coral trail", "Puddle Hopper", "2 of 3 goals done", plus an Unlocks list with ranks (Fledgling, Puddle Hopper, Spray Chaser, Wave Skimmer…). That's three parallel naming systems (level number, rank title, reward) dumped on me at once. **A ghosted gameplay layer (ring, bird, spray) shows through behind "Finish 3 goals…"**, which is a background bleed artefact. The left column (title at about y87, card at about y180) and the right column (UNLOCKS at about y60, first card at about y95) don't share a top line. The third goal and Level 5 are hard-clipped at the bottom with no fade or scroll cue.

## The four questions

1. **What do my fingers do?** I press and hold anywhere: one hold per wave. I hold while the bird runs down the face of a wave, let go at the bottom to launch, then hold again in the air to come down onto the next landing ring. It's not tapping; it's one timed press-and-release per wave. That's clear by s1-10. What isn't clear is what s1-02 "Hold it sideways" and s1-03/04 wanted from me.
2. **What is the storm?** It's a front chasing me from the left. The top-left bar shows how close it is. If it reaches me the run ends ("The storm caught you"). Perfect landings push it back.
3. **Where am I going?** Right, along a coast of named lighthouses at fixed distances (Gull Point 4 km, Saltlight 9, Kestrel Head 15, Old Harry 22, Wren Ness 30, "and on…"). I only really understood that from the Route screen after the run. In play, the storm bar has no destination end, and the "4 km" didn't count down.
4. **What is success and failure?** Success is landing Perfects in the gold ring, staying ahead of the storm, and passing lighthouses for distance. That feeds goals, then levels, then unlocks (birds, skies, trails). Failure is the storm catching me, which ends the run. The screens never show what an imperfect landing or a late release actually costs me. "Let go too soon" was just text.

## Verdicts

**App Store featuring quality?** Not yet. The art direction (sunset gradients, flat gull, layered sea, the Route chart) is genuinely in the right family. The core loop is legible and the Perfect/storm push-back is a good hook. But a featuring editor would hit s1-03, s1-04 and s1-14 in the first 60 seconds: two blank navy frames and a ghosted, rotated composite. That alone is disqualifying. Add a landing target hidden under Skip (s1-08), a "gold ring" caption with no ring (s1-12/13), and a label drawn through its own lighthouse, and it reads as a strong beta.

**What separates it from Alto / Monument Valley?** Those games never show a seam. Every transition is a continuous camera move, there's one type voice, one icon family and one button language, and the UI gets out of the way of the art. Swoop has:
- three hand-icon sizes in the tutorial
- two caption typefaces stacked on each other
- three lighthouse styles
- two navigation systems on home
- a home screen whose hierarchy changes between visits
- an economy (levels, ranks, goals, unlocks, daily, scores) that arrives all at once, after the fact, with no story

Alto also lets the world explain the goal: you see where you're going. Here the destination only makes sense on a menu screen after I've died.

**Do I feel I'm on a lesser version for not owning a foldable?** A bit, yes. The giant top-down spread-wing bird on home and in s1-02 does nothing on my phone. "The wings fold" and "Hold it sideways" describe something I'm clearly not doing. s1-03/s1-04 look like a step built for different hardware that got skipped or broken on mine. Nothing in actual play felt missing, but the wrapper keeps hinting that the real version is somewhere else. For £1.99 I don't want to feel that.

**Top 8 fixes (most important first)**
1. **s1-03 / s1-04 - remove the orientation glitch.** Lock the app to landscape from launch (landscape-only supported orientations, no portrait scene ever presented), and remove or skip the posture step on non-foldables. Replace the home-to-tutorial change with a single cross-fade or camera move. No frame should ever show the home UI ghosted over gameplay, or black letterboxing.
2. **s1-14 - fix the dead countdown frame.** Render the live scene behind the countdown and show actual 3-2-1 numerals, or remove the countdown. Never cut to a solid colour.
3. **s1-08 - the landing target must never sit under a control.** During the tutorial, move page dots and Skip to the top-right (next to the HUD), or keep a no-target zone of about 320x160pt in the bottom-right. Stop the dotted guide at the ring, not at the button.
4. **s1-12 / s1-13 - caption timing and the label collision.** Show "Land in the gold ring" only once a ring is on screen, and aim the dotted line at it (right now it runs off the left edge at nothing). Place lighthouse name labels above the sprite's top with an 8pt gap, so "Gull Point" isn't split by the tower.
5. **s1-01 / s1-02 / s1-17 - phone-specific copy and art.** On non-foldables, change "Hold it sideways" to "Hold anywhere" and drop "The wings fold". Either remove the top-down spread-wing bird or give it a role (for example, it tucks while you hold). Keep the home hierarchy identical between first and later visits: same instruction line, same tagline style.
6. **s1-15 / s1-11 - make the HUD say where I'm going.** Put a lighthouse marker at the right end of the storm bar so it reads "storm, you, next lighthouse". Make the next-lighthouse pill count down remaining distance ("3.9 km to go", 0.1 km steps). Give the "NEXT" tag 4pt of clearance above the pill.
7. **s1-16 - results hierarchy and the level debut.** Pick one hero: make "12.8 km" the hero and drop the headline to about 60% of its size, in white. Vertically centre the left card's content, or show the tern at 2-3x. Introduce levels with one line ("3 goals = a level. Level 2 unlocked: Tern"). Give "Choose Tern" the same button style as Fly again's secondary buttons. Add text labels or tooltips to the Home and Share icons.
8. **s1-17 / s1-18 / s1-19 - one design system for the menus.**
   - Home: merge Settings/Scores into the tile row (or the reverse) so there's one button language, fold Scores into Goals or rename it, and stop particles drawing over buttons.
   - Route: use one lighthouse icon set, with state shown by colour or opacity only, and remove the double capsule around Fly.
   - Goals: blur or dim the gameplay layer so it doesn't bleed through, align both columns to one top line, and add a bottom fade or scroll cue where content is clipped.

## Scores

- Understood: **7/10**
- Feels finished: **4/10**
- Keep playing: **6/10**
