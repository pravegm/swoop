## P0
1. **The lesson never says that Perfects push the storm back.** All three testers learned it only on Results.
   - In the storm step, after a Perfect the storm visibly recoils, the meter knob jumps away from the storm, and the
     words "Perfect pushes it back" sit by the meter.
   - In the land step the word **PERFECT** lands big when the player gets it.
   - Cutting the words went one step too far for non-gamers: keep the in-world word count low, but these two
     concepts must be stated.
2. **The goal sentence before the first run.** "Stay ahead of the storm. Reach the lighthouses." must show on the
   first real run's countdown (s1-14) and on Home before the first flight, not only after it (s1-17).
3. **The hint diagrams** (s02–s10).
   - Use one diagram style throughout.
   - The picture must show the pose its word asks for: "Fold a little" shows the folded target, not the same open
     book as "Hold it open".
   - The step-10 rhythm screen has too many symbols at once: show one cue.
4. **Clipping and contradictions** in Results, Home and Goals (s16, s17, s19). Tester 10 has the details.
   - On Goals, the level, rank and unlock names are meaningless to non-gamers. Show plain progress ("Level 2 of 15 ·
     next: New bird"), and fold the names into subtitles.
